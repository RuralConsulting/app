# Privacy and terms

## About this platform

Rural Consulting Limited (RCL) runs this platform so that farms and catchment groups can see their own maps and issued documents. This note explains what we hold, who can see it, and the terms for using it.

It applies to everyone who signs in at app.ruralconsulting.co.nz. We handle personal information under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

## What we hold

We hold only what is needed to show you your maps and files.

- Your account: your name, email address and sign-in details. Your password is never visible to us.
- Your farm's or group's maps: map layers, boundaries, farm features, drone imagery and photos that RCL publishes for you.
- Your issued documents: finished documents such as your Farm Environment Plan, main consents and farm maps. Drafts and working files stay with RCL and are not put on the platform.
- Records of changes: who published or changed something, and when.

We do not use advertising or tracking tools on this platform.

## Who can see it

Only people who have been given access to your farm or group can see its maps and files.

- Farm members see that farm's maps and files.
- Group members see that group's maps and files. A catchment group does not see a member farm's own Farm Environment Plan or other private files.
- Member farms can see the layers their catchment group has chosen to share, and no other group material.
- RCL staff can see and manage information so that we can do the work you have engaged us for. Staff need a second sign-in code to publish.

We do not sell your information. We only pass it to anyone else if you ask us to or the law requires it.

## Where it is kept

The platform's information is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in its Sydney, Australia data centre. Files are kept in private storage and opened through links that expire.

We keep your information for as long as you use the platform and as long as we need it for our work with you or to meet legal requirements. When a document is reissued, the older issue stays available and is marked as superseded.

## Your rights and who to contact

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. To do either, or to ask us to remove your access, contact your Rural Consulting advisor.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

## Terms of use

By signing in, you agree to these terms.

- Keep your sign-in to yourself. Your account is for you only.
- Maps are a guide. Map layers, boundaries and imagery come from a range of sources and may not be exact. Do not rely on them for legal boundaries or engineering decisions.
- The issued document is the record. Where a map and an issued document differ, the issued document applies. Check that a file is not marked as superseded.
- Use what you see for your own farm or group. Please do not pass on information belonging to another farm or group.
- Access can change. RCL may change or remove access, including when someone leaves a farm or group.
